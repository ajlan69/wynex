Domain & hosting

Your website. Your domain.

Your domain is purchased in your own name, so you keep ownership of your web address — the link that ends up on your CV, your Instagram bio and your business card.

You buy the domain
We guide you through it. You pay the registrar directly, at their cost — nothing is added on top, and it stays yours.
Hosted on your own account
Wherever possible, your website is set up on your own free Cloudflare Pages account, so you stay in control of it.
If that isn’t possible
Alternative hosting arrangements are handled with a written agreement, so both sides know the cost and how to exit.
You can always move it
Your domain and your website files are yours. Moving to another host later is your right, not a favour.
What about fonts, images and other paid services?

Your design and the code written for you become yours to use, edit and host anywhere once the final 50% is paid. A few things stay with whoever owns them:

  • Stock photos, icons and paid plugins keep their own licences.
  • Your domain and hosting are billed to you by the provider, and renewals are your responsibility.
  • Google Fonts and similar free fonts are fine to use; anything paid is yours to keep using on your own accounts.
  • We may show your site in our portfolio unless you’d rather we didn’t — just say so.

FAQ

Questions before you spend anything.

Answered honestly and briefly. If yours isn’t here, WhatsApp is the fastest way to get an answer.

Do I need to buy a domain?

Yes, and it’s cheap — usually a few hundred rupees a year. I’ll guide you through buying it in your own name. The registrar charges you directly, and that cost isn’t part of the package price.

Who owns my domain?

You do, completely. It’s registered in your name. WYNEX never takes ownership of a client domain, and never asks for your registrar password.

Where will my website be hosted?

Usually on your own free Cloudflare Pages account, set up with you so you stay in control. If that isn’t possible, WYNEX hosting can be arranged under a written agreement. You can move your site later either way.

How does the 50/50 payment work?

50% advance by UPI starts the work on your project. The remaining 50% is due only when the website is delivered and live — so you never pay the full amount before seeing the result.

How fast will I receive the website?

It depends on the package: Starter about 48 hours, Professional 3–5 days, Premium 5–7 days. The clock starts once we have your content, links and the 50% advance — and the date in your written scope is the one that counts. If content takes longer to gather, the date moves with it, and we tell you before you pay.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Two rounds of revisions are included in every package. Send changes over WhatsApp or email, consolidated in one message, and you’ll always know which round we’re on.

What if I don’t know what design I want?

That’s the normal starting point. Send your work and a couple of sites you like or dislike. A visual direction is proposed and approved before anything gets built.

Can you help me with website content?

Yes. Send rough notes, a résumé, project lists or even voice points, and they get shaped into proper About, project and contact sections. You approve every word before the site goes live.

Will the website work on mobile?

Yes — it’s designed phone-first, because most visitors will open your link from Instagram, WhatsApp or LinkedIn on a phone. Layouts are tested on small phones, tablets and desktops.

Can I use the website on my CV?

Yes — that’s the point of it. One link that works in your CV, your email signature and any application form.

Can you help with the domain and hosting setup?

Yes. We talk you through buying the domain in your own name, and set the site up on your Cloudflare account so you stay in control. Hosting and domain costs go to the provider directly, at their price.

Can I share it on Instagram and WhatsApp?

Yes. The link preview — the title, description and image that appear when a link is shared — is set up, so it looks right in an Instagram bio, a WhatsApp chat, LinkedIn or a DM.

What happens after the website is delivered?

You get the live website plus seven days of free support for bugs and small fixes. After that, further changes are handled as a small one-off quote. Your domain and website files stay yours.

Refund & revision policies
