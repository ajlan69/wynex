FAQ

Questions that remove doubt.

If your question isn’t here, WhatsApp is the fastest way to get an answer.

Do I need to buy a domain?

Yes, but it’s simple and cheap — usually a few hundred rupees a year. We guide you through buying it, and it stays in your name. The domain cost goes to the registrar, not to WYNEX, and isn’t included in the package prices.

Who owns my domain?

You do — 100%. It’s purchased on your account in your name. WYNEX never takes ownership and never asks for your registrar password.

Where will my website be hosted?

Preferably on your own free Cloudflare Pages account — we set it up with you so you stay the owner. If you don’t have one, we can host it on WYNEX hosting under a written agreement covering cost and exit. You can move it anytime.

How long does it take?

Starter sites are targeted within 48 hours of receiving your content and the 50% advance. Professional takes around 3–5 days, Premium around 5–7 days. The clock starts when we have everything we need from you.

How does payment work?

By UPI: 50% advance confirms your slot and starts the work, 50% is due when the site is delivered and live. No full upfront payment, no hidden charges.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Two rounds of revisions are included in every package, tracked over WhatsApp or email so both sides can see where things stand. Further changes are quoted before starting — never billed afterwards without your approval.

What if I don’t know what I want my website to look like?

That’s normal — most clients don’t. Send your work, a few links you like (or dislike), and your field. We propose a visual direction for approval before building anything.

Can you help me write the content?

Yes. Send rough points — your background, work, links — and we’ll shape them into clean About, project and contact sections. You approve every word before it goes live.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes — it’s designed phone-first, because most visitors will open it from Instagram, WhatsApp or LinkedIn on their phone. We test on small phones, tablets and desktops before handover.

Can I use my website on my CV, LinkedIn and Instagram?

That’s exactly what it’s for. One short link works everywhere — CV header, LinkedIn profile, Instagram bio, WhatsApp status, email signature. We also set up the link preview so it looks good when shared.

Can I contact you after the website is delivered?

Yes. Seven days of free support for bugs and small fixes after launch, over WhatsApp or email. After that, changes are a small one-off quote. Your domain stays yours either way.

What is your refund policy?

If we miss the agreed delivery date and you haven’t approved a design, you get a full refund of what you paid. Once design is approved and building has started, we refund the advance minus completed work, with the breakdown shown to you. Full wording is in the Terms and the policy section below.

Refund policy

If we cannot deliver by the agreed date and no design is approved, full refund — usually within 5 working days. After design approval, refund minus completed work, with breakdown shown first.

Revision policy

Two rounds included per package, tracked in writing. Further rounds or direction changes are quoted before starting — never billed silently.
